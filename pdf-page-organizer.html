Frequently asked questions

Is this better than the other page tools?

It is the easiest when you aren’t sure which pages need changing. For exact page lists, Extract, Delete or Rotate are faster.

Will it change the quality of my pages?

Pages are rearranged, not redrawn, so quality stays the same.

Is PDF Page Organizer really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no watermark and no paid plan. The site is supported by ads.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Your file is processed by code running in your browser. The page only downloads the open-source libraries it needs (for OCR, also the language data), never your file.
