✨ Ask PDFSnap

🔒 Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Things you can ask

🗂️ “Merge these 3 PDFs and compress to under 1 MB”
✂️ “Split pages 2 to 4”
🗑️ “Delete page 3 and add page numbers”
🔃 “Rotate all pages 90° clockwise”
🖼️ “Convert this PDF to JPG”
📑 “Make these photos into one PDF”
📐 “Resize to 800 px and convert to PNG”
🗜️ “Compress each image under 100 KB”

How it works

  1. Attach your filesTap the + button and pick PDFs or images from your phone or computer.
  2. Say what you wantType it the way you’d say it, or tap an example. You can chain steps, like “merge these, then compress to under 1 MB”.
  3. Download the resultPDFSnap shows what it understood, does the work on your device, and gives you a download button.

Good to know

  • It is not an AI model. It understands a set range of requests using rules in your browser, which is why your files and messages never leave your device.
  • To reach a small target size, PDF pages may be saved as images, so the text can’t be selected afterwards. It tells you when that happens.
  • Password protection, signing, OCR and cropping have their own tools, and it points you to them.
  • Very large files can be slow on older phones.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ask PDFSnap an AI chatbot?

No. It is a built-in assistant that understands a defined set of requests (merge, split, compress, rotate, convert and so on) using rules that run in your browser. No AI service is involved, so your messages and files are never sent anywhere. If it doesn’t understand you, it says so and shows examples.

Are my files or messages uploaded?

No. Your files are processed by code running on your own device. The page only downloads the open-source PDF libraries it needs, never your files or what you type.

What can I ask it to do?

Merge PDFs (and photos) into one, split by page ranges or every N pages, extract or delete pages, rotate, reverse pages, add page numbers or a text watermark, compress PDFs and images (even to a size such as “under 200 KB”), convert PDF to JPG or PNG, turn images into a PDF, resize and flip images, and make files black and white. You can chain steps in one sentence.

Why couldn’t it reach the size I asked for?

Some files simply can’t get that small without becoming unreadable, for example PDFs full of photos. In that case it gets as close as it can and tells you the result, so you can decide.

Does compressing a PDF change the text?

To reach a small target size, PDF pages may be saved as images, which means the text can no longer be selected or searched. If you simply ask it to “compress” a text-based PDF, it optimizes the file without changing how it looks.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It is designed for phones first. Tap the + button to pick files from your phone, then type or tap an example.
